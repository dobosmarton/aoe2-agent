# Imperial Age — Win the Game

Imperial Age is the endgame. You have access to all units and technologies. Focus on destroying the enemy.

## Imperial Age Checklist (in addition to universal checklist)

**Villager queuing:** past Feudal there is no fixed order target — only the food gate caps queuing, so the numbers below are a heuristic, not a hard stop.
- **Population < 130**: Keep producing villagers to replace losses and maintain economy.
- **Population 130+**: Stop producing. Focus entirely on military production and upgrades.

**Villager allocation:** 20+ on food, 12-15 on wood, 8-10 on gold, 4-6 on stone. Trade Carts supplement gold.

## Key Priorities

1. **Research elite upgrades** at Castle for unique units. These are massive power spikes.
2. **Build Trebuchets** (Siege Workshop → R, or Castle → W, 200 wood + 200 gold): Only unit that outranges Castles and TCs. Protect them with army.
3. **Set up trade routes**: Build Trade Carts at Market (Ctrl-M → Q). They generate infinite gold between your Market and an allied Market or your own distant Market. Essential for late-game gold income.
4. **Research all Blacksmith upgrades** — fully upgraded units are dramatically stronger.
5. **Build multiple Castles** for map control and unique unit production.
6. **Maintain army production** from 4-6 military buildings. Never stop producing.

## Army Composition

Mix unit types to counter the enemy:
- **Knights/Cavalier/Paladin** (Stable): Strong vs archers and siege. Weak vs spearmen and monks.
- **Arbalest** (Archery Range): Strong vs infantry. Weak vs cavalry and siege.
- **Halberdier** (Barracks): Strong vs cavalry. Cheap, fast to produce.
- **Trebuchet** (Castle/Siege Workshop): Destroys buildings from long range. Must unpack to fire, vulnerable while moving.
- **Unique Units** (Castle): Civilization-specific. Usually the strongest option.
- **Siege Ram** (Siege Workshop → Q): Tanks arrow fire, destroys buildings. Garrison infantry inside for protection and bonus damage.

## Winning the Game

- Destroy ALL enemy Town Centers, Castles, and military buildings.
- Use Trebuchets to break fortified positions.
- Keep your economy running — late-game battles are wars of attrition.
- If gold runs out, switch to trash units (Halberdier, Skirmisher, Light Cavalry) which cost no gold.
- Trade Carts are your lifeline for gold in long games.
