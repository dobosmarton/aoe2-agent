# Castle Age — 50% Economy, 50% Military

Castle Age is the power spike. You need to boom your economy with additional Town Centers while building a real army.

## Castle Age Checklist (in addition to universal checklist)

**Villager queuing:** past Feudal there is no fixed order target — only the food gate caps queuing, so treat the numbers below as a heuristic, not a hard stop.
- **Population < 80**: Keep producing villagers from ALL Town Centers. Build 1-2 additional TCs.
- **Population 80+**: Slow down. Focus military production. Only replace lost villagers.

> **Build execution:** always use the `build` composite — it opens whichever menu the building lives in, given a menu-qualified `building_key` (bare letter = economic menu, `wq`=Barracks, `vd`=Market). Never hand-roll `.` → menu → key → click: selecting the villager moves the camera, so your placement coordinate is stale before the click lands (F-33). Town Centers, University, Castle, Towers and walls are **not wired yet** — their V-menu slots are unverified and several cost stone, so a wrong slot is expensive. Build what's wired and spend the rest on units and upgrades.

**Villager allocation:** 15-18 on food, 10-12 on wood, 6-8 on gold, 3-4 on stone (if building Castles/TCs).

**Economy boom — Build additional Town Centers:**
- Extra Town Centers (275 wood + 100 stone) are the standard boom, but their
  build slot is **not wired yet** — don't attempt the manual sequence. Put the
  banked wood into farms, military buildings and upgrades instead.
- Queue villagers at the TC you have, constantly.

**Military production:**
- Build 2-3 military production buildings (Archery Range, Stable, Siege Workshop).
- Train army constantly. Idle military buildings = wasted potential.
- **Knights** (Stable → W, 60 food + 75 gold): Strong all-purpose cavalry. Best unit in early Castle Age.
- **Crossbowmen** (Archery Range → Q, 25 wood + 45 gold): Upgrade from archers. Good in large groups.
- **Monks** (Monastery → Q, 100 gold): Convert enemy units. Heal your army.
- **Siege** (Siege Workshop): Mangonel (W) for grouped enemies, Ram (Q) for buildings.

**Castle:** Build a Castle when you have 650 stone (Villager → V → C). Unlocks unique units and Trebuchets.

**Imperial Age transition:** Food >= 1000 AND gold >= 800 → Research Imperial Age (H → Z).
- Requires 2 Castle Age buildings (University, Siege Workshop, or Castle).
- University (Villager → V → G, 200 wood) is the easiest to build.

## Key Priorities

1. Build additional TCs immediately — this is the #1 Castle Age priority
2. Start Knight production if you have a Stable
3. Keep farming — you need massive food income for Knights + villagers + Imperial Age
4. Mine gold with 6-8 villagers — Knights and Imperial Age need gold
5. Mine stone with 3-4 villagers if planning Castles or TCs
6. Research upgrades at Blacksmith (attack + armor for your main unit type)
7. Wall your base if not already walled

## Defense

- Use military to defend. Garrison villagers only as last resort.
- Build Towers (Villager → V → F, 125 wood + 25 stone) at vulnerable spots.
- If being raided: pull villagers to TC, garrison if needed, counter-attack with army.
- Build walls (Villager → V → S for palisade, D for stone wall) to protect economy.
